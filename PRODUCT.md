## Stack

## Users

- **Confirmed:** recruiters and hiring managers assessing Kaustubh's engineering work, personal point of view, and creative design taste.
- Their primary job is to understand who Kaustubh is, recognize how he thinks and builds, explore the strongest projects, and open a live demo without searching through decorative UI.

## Product Purpose

The site introduces Kaustubh as an AI systems builder, demonstrates his creative design taste, and makes his working style and strongest projects memorable. Success means a recruiter or hiring manager can quickly understand the person, the project problem, the engineering approach, and the available proof, then explore a project and open its live demo.

## Positioning

The portfolio combines serious AI-systems work with one continuous, hand-inked comic world. Its distinctive mechanism is a persistent procedural scene whose scroll, cursor, copy, and interface materials all advance the same physical narrative.

## Operating Context

- Visitors may arrive on desktop or mobile from a resume, social profile, or direct link.
- The home page is an overview; the planned projects route is a deeper archive for five selected builds.
- Project records need a title, short problem-and-solution summary, replaceable project image, a prominent live-demo action, and a secondary source or case-study action.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing home page while adding a progressive-enhancement `My Projects` link and a dedicated projects page.
- The requested route transition uses one causal crush/fracture/tear sequence; navigation must still work if JavaScript, WebGL, or motion is unavailable.
- The projects page must support five semantic project checkpoints and comparable scroll craft without adding a second permanent render loop per project.
- AlterScore and Pathfinder are confirmed project names and summaries in the existing portfolio. Records three through five remain placeholders and must not be presented as verified case studies.
- Demo, case-study, and repository destinations are undecided unless a real URL already exists; previews must not fabricate them.
- Project exploration and live-demo opens outrank decorative interaction. The cinematic layer must create anticipation, then clear space for fast scanning and direct actions.
- **Open decision:** choose the storm-forged (Thor-adjacent) or pressure-faultline (Hulk-adjacent) direction after reviewing the concept previews.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Kaustubh.
- Existing voice: direct, technical, energetic, and handmade rather than corporate or generic.
- **Confirmed tone:** cinematic and playful. Playfulness comes from physical comic timing and authored interaction, not novelty copy, gamification, or visual clutter.
- Preserve the incumbent comic-print identity, procedural city continuity, and role-based typography.
- **Inferred deployment constraint:** superhero influence should be expressed through original materials, forces, and motion—not licensed character likenesses, logos, quotes, costumes, or copied weapons.

## Product Principles

1. Project evidence stays more legible and important than spectacle.
2. One physical metaphor coordinates the scene, scroll progress, transition, and micro-interactions.
3. Every primary destination remains a real semantic link with an honest fallback.
4. Placeholder work is labelled as placeholder work; no screenshots, metrics, outcomes, or URLs are invented.
5. Desktop craft, mobile composition, reduced motion, keyboard use, and CDN/WebGL failure are designed as first-class states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

**Inferred baseline:** preserve keyboard navigation and visible focus, readable contrast, semantic links and headings, coarse-pointer support, and a true reduced-motion path with no large camera movement, fragmentation, or continuous decorative motion.
