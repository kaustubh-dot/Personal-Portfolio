# Project-first comic portfolio

The root page keeps its Spider-Man / Marvel-inspired comic identity and persistent procedural city. All three project entries remain explicitly labelled placeholders.

## Current direction

- Every main section has a transparent background. No cream section overlays, blur layers, or hero gradients obscure the city.
- Bangers display lettering, red and blue accents, ink outlines, speech balloons, and web graphics connect the foreground to the existing background.
- Three large comic-cover placeholders alternate across the scene. They have no invented claims or destination links.
- The introduction, about text, and contact links are kept concise so projects carry the page.
- The red loading entrance uses a web-and-eyes emblem and moving rule, with a bounded dismissal time and no fabricated percentage.

## Motion

The procedural city, rooftop figure, moon, scroll-driven web, camera movement, cursor following, trail, magnetic links, click shots, comic bursts, and web splats remain. Project covers have a light pointer tilt.

The scene initializes separately from the content animations. Missing CDN libraries, unavailable WebGL, disabled JavaScript, and reduced-motion preferences leave the portfolio readable. Reduced motion disables decorative click and pointer effects.

## Replacing the placeholders

The three article elements in index.html are the project slots. Replace their title and copy, and swap assets/images/project-01.svg, project-02.svg, or project-03.svg for project media. Set width and height to match replacement images. Add semantic links when real destinations are available.

The preview/ concept pages remain separate from the root page.
