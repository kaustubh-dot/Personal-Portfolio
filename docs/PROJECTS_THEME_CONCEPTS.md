Hulk-adjacent energy is expressed through mass, compression, braced slabs, a bruise-indigo pressure field, one restrained gamma-lime faultline, and an abstract primitive impact mass. It does not use an actor likeness, costume, radioactive prop, logo, or catchphrase.

## Shared route transition

One causal handoff with theme-specific physics:

1. The semantic `My Projects` link depresses at its real screen position.
2. Storm-Forged drives a forged tool and authored bolts into that origin; Gamma Faultline drives an abstract impact mass into it and shakes the camera.
3. The newsprint foreground separates into deterministic Three.js plates.
4. The plates cover the old page, navigation occurs, and the chosen destination reads the short-lived session arrival state.
5. The destination performs its own mandatory forge/breach loader over the live Three.js world.
6. Only after that destination impact completes does the page reveal, the arrival state clear, and normal scrolling continue.

The route remains a real link. Reduced motion uses a short non-physical handoff. Back/forward navigation clears stale covers and cancels the link transform.

## Decision rule

- Choose **Storm-Forged Archive** for the stronger cinematic continuation, more playful physical timing, and more memorable demonstration of creative design taste.
- Choose **Gamma Faultline** for the clearest project browsing, strongest transition causality, and slightly lower implementation risk.
- Do not merge their permanent world metaphors. The shared crush-to-tear route transition can lead into either one.

With the confirmed recruiter audience and cinematic-playful tone, **Storm-Forged Archive is the recommended direction**. Its conduit is a natural evolution of the home page's web, so it demonstrates continuity and creative authorship rather than introducing a disconnected themed microsite. The implementation must keep the live-demo action visually dominant once a project becomes active.
